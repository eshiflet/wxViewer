Thermal Phase: outdoor (lagged) vs indoor °F ℹ
Thermal Phase Scatter
Outdoor temp (shifted by thermal lag) vs indoor temp at that moment.

How to read it:
• X-axis — outdoor °F, smoothed & shifted back by the lag hours
• Y-axis — indoor °F at that moment
• Dashed line — regression fit: what indoor temp should be from outdoor alone
• ■ Orange — heater running (indoor warmer than predicted)
• ■ Blue — A/C running (indoor cooler than predicted)
• ■ Gray — HVAC idle, house tracking outdoor naturally

The physics:
• Tight cluster along line = good coupling, HVAC off
• Slope = thermal coupling (indoor °F per outdoor °F)
• Wide scatter = heavy HVAC use fighting the outdoor temp
• Flat line = thermally decoupled (typical for basements)
